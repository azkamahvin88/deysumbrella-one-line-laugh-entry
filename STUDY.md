## What the judge brief says (RULES.txt, read in full)

Judge: AI model z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, head-to-head two lines at a time, no ties,
margin 1–5. Persona: deadpan comic who has heard every joke twice.
Criteria, most important first:
1. A funny one-line joke about anything at all; a line that is not funny, or a
   caption/stray thought with no joke, loses to any funny line.
2. The laugh comes from the line itself — a turn, a surprise, or one exact word —
   not from explaining the joke or leaning on a familiar meme.
3. One line, short enough to read in a breath, no setup paragraph, no second
   line doing the work.
4. Fresh wording, a joke the reader has not seen a hundred times, in plain
   words that sound like a person talking.
5. Lands on a single read, with no footnote, no context, no shared knowledge.
Dev: each valid entry is judged against 3 house anchors + 3 rungs; top 500 of
the dev board reach the final heat (Swiss + playoff). Tie-break: playoff
head-to-head, then SHORTER text (fewer bytes), then earlier receipt — so a
short line submitted early has an edge. Byte-identical entries: only the
earliest receipt can win.

## Why #4 won

- Criterion 1: a real joke, not a caption — a request and an answer.
- Criterion 2: the laugh is the turn itself: the universe answers literally,
  and the answer is a parking ticket, the most bureaucratic reply imaginable.
- Criterion 3: 57 characters, one breath, no setup, no second joke.
- Criterion 4: the "universe for a sign" setup is familiar, but the parking
  ticket payoff is specific and not the usual answer.
- Criterion 5: needs nothing — no meme, no context, no footnote.
- Tie-break: 57 chars is among the shortest competitive lines, and the field
  check found no exact match ("parking" appears in 1 of 187 titles, "sign" in 3,
  never together as this joke).
